# Integration Boundary

OpenLine Reports intentionally exposes a small contract that other systems can satisfy without adopting internal implementation details.

Required concepts:

- investigation
- question
- initial hypothesis
- source
- claim
- basis/dependency
- objection
- uncertainty
- discovery delta
- disposition
- correction/reopening

A provenance or claim-graph system may sit underneath this package, but the public interface does not require a specific implementation.

A writing or publication system may sit above this package, but it must treat research output as input rather than allowing prose to promote evidence.
