# Example reports

These reports show one way the `openline-reports` research harness can feed a downstream editorial workflow.

They are examples, not part of the core validation contract. The harness produces bounded research states; a downstream user decides how to turn those states into an article, memo, experiment proposal, investment review, or other output.

## Reports

1. [The Friend Who Needs Nothing From You](001-the-friend-who-needs-nothing-from-you.md)
2. [The Better You Get at Dating Apps, the Worse the Date Can Get](002-the-better-you-get-at-dating-apps-the-worse-the-date-can-get.md)
3. [The Customer Is About to Leave the Store](003-the-customer-is-about-to-leave-the-store.md)

Each report includes the source list used during drafting. The examples are included to make the research-to-output boundary concrete, not to claim that the repository certifies prose or publication decisions.
